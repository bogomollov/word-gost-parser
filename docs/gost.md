Student Paper Formatting Specifications

1. Page Setup & Sheet Layout

font family: Times New Roman

font size: not applicable

font style: regular

first line indent: not applicable

alignment: not applicable

line spacing: not applicable

space before: 0 pt

space after: 0 pt

page placement: standard A4 sheet (210 x 297 mm)

margins (frameless): left 30 mm, right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm

margins (frame template): left 20 mm, right 5 mm, top 5 mm, bottom 5 mm

mandatory rules: single-sided printing, black ink only, inner frame padding min 10 mm top/bottom and min 3 mm sides

2. Page Numbers

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 14 pt

font style: regular

first line indent: 0 mm

alignment: center

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 0 pt

space after: 0 pt

page placement: bottom center of sheet

margins: within standard bottom margin (20 mm from edge)

mandatory rules: Arabic numerals only, no trailing dot, no dashes, counted from sheet 1 (title sheet hidden), continuous through appendices

3. Title Page (Front Page)

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 14 pt (main data), 16 pt (document title and work type)

font style: bold (work type and title), regular (university header and metadata)

first line indent: 0 mm

alignment: center (headers, titles), left (student and supervisor data block)

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 0 pt

space after: 0 pt

page placement: sheet 1

margins: standard left 30 mm (or 20 mm framed), right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm

mandatory rules: no hyphenation anywhere, no trailing dot on titles, classification code mandatory, all signatures signed in blue ink only

4. Assignment Sheet

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 16 pt (main title), 14 pt (subtitle), 12 pt (body and form fields)

font style: bold (titles), regular (form fields)

first line indent: 0 mm (header and labels), 12.5 mm (body fields)

alignment: center (title), left or justified (tasks and inputs)

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 0 pt

space after: 0 pt

page placement: sheet 2 (immediately after title page)

margins: left min 20 mm for binding, right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm

mandatory rules: page number hidden, issued and signed by supervisor, signed in blue ink

5. Abstract / Annotation

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 16 pt (heading), 14 pt (body)

font style: bold (heading), regular (body)

first line indent: 0 mm (heading), 12.5 mm (body text)

alignment: center (heading), justified (body text)

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 0 pt (heading), 2 line spaces (body text relative to heading)

space after: 2 line spaces (heading)

page placement: sheet 3 (always starts on a new sheet)

margins: left 30 mm (or 20 mm framed), right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm

mandatory rules: length limited to 0.33 - 0.5 of A4 sheet, must conclude with total sheet/figure/table count, engineering sheets include title block at bottom

6. Table of Contents

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 16 pt (heading), 14 pt (entries)

font style: bold (heading), regular (entries)

first line indent: 0 mm (heading and main entries), 12.5 mm (sub-entries)

alignment: center (heading), left-justified with right-aligned page numbers (entries)

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 0 pt

space after: 2 line spaces below heading

page placement: always starts on a new sheet (after abstract)

margins: left 30 mm (or 20 mm framed), right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm

mandatory rules: dot leader between title text and page number, no trailing dots after page numbers, all chapters/sections/appendices listed

7. Main Section Headings (Chapters)

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 16 pt

font style: bold

first line indent: 12.5 mm

alignment: left

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 0 pt (new sheet)

space after: 2 line spaces (to text) or 1 line space (to subsection heading)

page placement: always starts on a new sheet

margins: left 30 mm (or 20 mm framed), right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm

mandatory rules: no trailing dot after section number or title text, no hyphenation, no underlining, max 4 numbering levels

8. Subsection Headings

font family: Times New Roman  
 
font size: 14 pt 
  
font style: bold

first line indent: 12.5 mm
   
alignment: left
   
line spacing: single (1.0)
   
space before: 2 line spaces (from preceding text) or 1 line space (from parent section heading)
   
space after: 2 line spaces (to following text) 
  
page placement: continuous flow within current sheet   

margins: left 30 mm (or 20 mm framed), right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm  
 
mandatory rules: no trailing dot after subsection number or title text, no hyphenation, no underlining, minimum number of sources is not fixed by this standard and must be clarified via department methodological guidelines

9. Body Text

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 14 pt

font style: regular

first line indent: 12.5 mm (equal to 5 characters of 14 pt)

alignment: justified

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 0 pt

space after: 0 pt

page placement: continuous flow within sections

margins: left 30 mm (or 20 mm framed), right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm

mandatory rules: automatic hyphenation enabled, comma used as decimal separator (e.g., 0,15), non-breaking space before measurement units, no minus signs before negative values in prose

10. Lists & Enumerations

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 14 pt

font style: regular

first line indent: 12.5 mm (first level), 12.5 mm + 2 spaces shift (second level)

alignment: justified

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 0 pt

space after: 0 pt

page placement: continuous flow within text

margins: left 30 mm (or 20 mm framed), right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm

mandatory rules: hyphen (-) used as primary bullet, lowercase Latin letter with parenthesis a) b) for referenced items, Arabic numerals with parenthesis 1) 2) for sub-items

11. Tables

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 14 pt (standard text), 12 pt (permissible for dense text)

font style: regular

first line indent: 12.5 mm (table label above table), 0 mm (inside cells)

alignment: left (table label), center or left (cell contents)

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 1 line space (between preceding text and table label)

space after: 1 line space (between table bottom border and following text)

page placement: continuous flow immediately after first reference or next sheet

margins: left and right table borders flush with main text margins

mandatory rules: label format "Table 1 - Title", no trailing dot after label/title, dedicated index column prohibited, row numbers placed in stub, split tables require continuation label on next sheet

12. Figures & Illustrations

font family: Times New Roman (captions and labels)

font size: 14 pt (caption and explanatory notes)

font style: regular

first line indent: 12.5 mm (or center aligned for caption)

alignment: center (image), left or center (caption)

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 1 line space (between preceding text and top of figure)

space after: 1 line space (between bottom of caption and following text)

page placement: continuous flow immediately after first mention or next sheet

margins: image fits within page text margins

mandatory rules: label format "Figure 1 - Title", no trailing dot after caption, min 300 DPI resolution, multi-element diagrams grouped into single object

13. Formulas & Equations

font family: Times New Roman / Symbol / Equation Editor

font size: 14 pt (main symbols), 10 pt (large indices), 8 pt (small indices), 20 pt (large operators), 14 pt (small operators)

font style: italic (variables), regular (constants, indices, numbers)

first line indent: 0 mm (centered equation), 0 mm (variable explanation block)

alignment: center (formula), right (numbering in parentheses)

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 1 line space (above formula)

space after: 1 line space (below formula)

page placement: continuous flow on dedicated line

margins: formula centered between page margins, equation number flush right

mandatory rules: numbered with Arabic numerals in parentheses (1) or (1.1), variable explanations start on new line with word "where", no trailing dots after equation numbers

14. Notes & Explanations

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 12 pt

font style: regular

first line indent: 12.5 mm

alignment: justified

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 0 pt

space after: 0 pt

page placement: placed immediately below referenced text or above table bottom border

margins: left 30 mm (or 20 mm framed), right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm

mandatory rules: word "Note" formatted with 2.5 pt expanded tracking, single note label format "Note - Text", multiple notes numbered 1, 2 without dash

15. Footnotes

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 12 pt

font style: regular

first line indent: 0 mm

alignment: justified

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: separated by short horizontal divider line from main text

space after: 0 pt

page placement: bottom of current sheet (within page bounds)

margins: left 30 mm (or 20 mm framed), right 10 mm, bottom 20 mm

mandatory rules: footnote index marked as superscript number with parenthesis 1) or asterisk *, max 3 asterisks per page, separate numbering per sheet, ends with period

16. References (Bibliography)

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 16 pt (heading), 14 pt (entries)

font style: bold (heading), regular (entries)

first line indent: 0 mm (heading), 12.5 mm (entries)

alignment: center (heading), justified (entries)

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 0 pt (new sheet)

space after: 2 line spaces below heading

page placement: always starts on a new sheet

margins: left 30 mm (or 20 mm framed), right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm

mandatory rules: Arabic numeral numbering without trailing dot, in-text citation in square brackets [1], ordered strictly by order of appearance in text, GOST bibliographic standards

17. Appendices

font family: Times New Roman

font size: 16 pt (appendix label), 14 pt (status label and title)

font style: bold (label and title), bold italic (status label)

first line indent: 0 mm

alignment: center

line spacing: single (1.0)

space before: 0 pt (new sheet)

space after: 1 line space between labels, 2 line spaces below title to appendix content

page placement: each appendix starts on a brand-new sheet

margins: left 30 mm (or 20 mm framed), right 10 mm, top 20 mm, bottom 20 mm

mandatory rules: uppercase Latin letters used for indexing (Appendix A), status label enclosed in parentheses like (Mandatory) or (Informational), internal tables/figures/formulas numbered with appendix letter prefix (Table A.1, Figure A.1, Formula A.1)
